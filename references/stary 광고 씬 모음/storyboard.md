# STARY Commercial Storyboard

**Project:** STARY – Leave Your Memories Among the Stars  
**Duration:** Approximately 15 seconds  
**Style:** Emotional cinematic commercial / Realistic live-action

---

# Creative Direction

This commercial tells the story of a man who discovers that every star above the city is someone's memory.

The stars are not magical objects.
They are precious moments that people chose to preserve.

The protagonist realizes this after seeing the stars in the sky.
Inspired by them, he decides to preserve his own moment.

His memory does not become light.

Instead, **the scenery he is looking at becomes a new star**, symbolizing that every diary entry can remain in the universe forever.

The overall tone should resemble premium emotional commercials by Apple or Google.

Avoid fantasy-like visual effects.
Every movement should feel subtle, believable and cinematic.

The emotional flow is:

**Curiosity → Wonder → Nostalgia → Realization → Hope**

# Scene 10

**Image**
scene7.png

**Duration**
3 seconds

**Description**

The audience returns to the riverside at night.

The man quietly looks at the same city skyline once more.

He gently raises one hand.

Instead of creating light from his hand, **the scenery he is looking at begins to glow**.

The river.

The skyline.

The night.

The reflections.

Everything within his current view slowly lifts away from reality as countless tiny particles.

The entire moment transforms into a single beautiful star.

That new star rises into the night sky and quietly joins the other stars already watching over the city.

The audience now understands:

Every star is someone's preserved memory.

Now his memory has become one as well.

The camera slowly pulls back, revealing the city beneath a sky that now holds one more story.

# Visual Direction

- Realistic live-action only.
- Premium cinematic advertisement.
- Apple-like emotional storytelling.
- Smooth, restrained camera movement.
- No exaggerated magical explosions.
- Minimal visual effects.
- Stars symbolize memories, not fantasy.
- Warm memories should contrast naturally with the cool night city.
- Prioritize emotion through visuals rather than dialogue.
- Every transition should feel seamless and dreamlike, as if moving through memories.
